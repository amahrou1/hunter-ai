Acme Corp Bug Bounty Program
Intigriti | Tier 2 | Responsible disclosure

Program description
Acme builds project management software. We welcome reports on the assets below.

In scope
Domain                       Type        Tier
*.acme-test.io               Wildcard    Tier 2
app.acme-test.com            Web app     Tier 1
https://api.acme-test.com/v2 API         Tier 1
com.acme.android             Android     Tier 3

Out of scope
status.acme-test.io          Third party (Statuspage)
*.staging.acme-test.io       Not production

Rules of engagement
- Do not exceed 10 requests per second.
- Include the header X-Intigriti-Username: <your username> in every request.
- Register test accounts only with your @intigriti.me email address.
- No denial of service, no social engineering, no physical attacks.

Not accepted
- Missing security headers
- Self-XSS
- Rate limiting on non-authentication endpoints
